Callandra Moore is a PhD student in Complex Systems and Data Science at the University of Vermont. She is a long-time social justice activist whose research interests are primarily in the use of quantitative and computational methods to advance research on human rights. She has a Bachelor of Applied Science in engineering physics and political science and a Master of Public Health in epidemiology, both from the University of Toronto.
