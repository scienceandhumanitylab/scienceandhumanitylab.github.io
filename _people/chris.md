Chris Donovan is a PhD student in Complex Systems and Data Science at the University of Vermont and serves as a Food Systems Data Scientist at the Food Systems Research Institute. His work involves food system sustainability research, on-farm research networks, and cultivating FAIR data, while his research pursuits include participatory food systems modeling and reproducible analytical workflows. Chris has a MS in Community Development and Applied Economics from UVM and a BS in Environmental Studies with an emphasis in Conservation Biology from Prescott College.
