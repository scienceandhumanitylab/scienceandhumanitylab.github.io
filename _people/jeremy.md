Jeremy Elliott is a PhD student in Complex Systems and Data Science at the University of Vermont, co-advised by Trisha Shrum in the department of Community Development and Applied Economics. His current research examines how story structure motivates pro-environmental behavior within a highly effective climate intervention. More broadly, he's interested in using data science to understand the drivers of pro-social behavior, and how to communicate data effectively. He holds a B.S. in Physics with Honors from UVM.
